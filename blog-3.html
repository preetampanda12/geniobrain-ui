Patent Renewal in India: Everything You Need to Know

By GenioBrain Experts • 5 Min Read

Securing a patent in India is a significant milestone, but the journey doesn't end with the grant. To keep your exclusive rights alive and enforceable, maintaining the patent through regular renewals is legally mandatory. Patent renewal in India ensures that your invention remains protected for its full term of 20 years.

When to File a Renewal?

In India, patent renewal fees (also known as maintenance fees) become payable only after the patent is granted. However, the fee is calculated retroactively from the 3rd year onwards, starting from the date of filing the patent application.

"Missing a patent renewal deadline can lead to the lapsing of your patent, throwing your valuable intellectual property into the public domain where anyone can use it freely."

The Timeline and Fees

The patent renewal fee must be paid annually. The payment window opens well before the due date, and the fee must be paid before the expiration of the relevant year. Here are the key things to remember:

  • Initial Payment: Upon grant, accumulated fees for the past years (from the 3rd year) must be paid within 3 months.
  • Subsequent Years: You must pay the renewal fee before the end of the preceding year.
  • Extension of Time: If you miss the deadline, you can still pay within a 6-month grace period, subject to an extension fee.
  • Restoration: If the patent lapses, you can file an application for restoration within 18 months from the date of lapse.

Working with an IP attorney to track deadlines and manage payments is highly recommended to avoid the accidental loss of your patent rights.
